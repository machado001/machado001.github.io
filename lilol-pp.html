Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-13

About this policy

This policy explains what information the Android app Lilol (package com.machado001.lilol) handles, why, and the choices you have. Lilol is developed by machado001, an individual developer based in Brazil.

Lilol has no user accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Game data

Champion, ability, skin and lore information comes from Riot Games' public Data Dragon service. This week's free champion rotation comes from Lilol's own server (Google Firebase Cloud Functions), which asks Riot Games for it. These requests contain no personal information about you. Like any internet request, they reveal your device's IP address to the server that answers them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Lilol is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google collects and processes:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location;
  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information about ad and app performance.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Lilol asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices inside the app.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

  • Firebase Analytics records how the app is used (for example, which screens are opened) together with device model, operating system version and an app instance ID, so we can understand what to improve.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report when the app crashes: the stack trace, the app's state and device information at that moment, and a Crashlytics installation ID.

This information is not linked to your name or any account, and we do not sell it.

Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

If you allow notifications, Lilol subscribes to the "new free rotation" topic. Firebase Cloud Messaging uses an installation token and the app version to deliver those messages. We do not store tokens on our server. Turning notifications off in Android settings stops the messages.

App integrity (Firebase App Check)

To protect Lilol's server from abuse, Firebase App Check uses Google Play Integrity to confirm that requests come from the genuine app.

Service providers and data transfers

The services above are provided by Google LLC, which may process data in countries other than your own, under Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with anyone else.

Retention and deletion

Lilol stores the latest rotation and your language preference only on your device; uninstalling the app removes them. Analytics, crash and advertising data are kept by Google according to its retention settings. Because Lilol has no accounts, we cannot link this data to you, but you may contact us with any request.

Children

Lilol is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when Lilol's data practices change and change the "Last updated" date above.
