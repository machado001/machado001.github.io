Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

Thank you for using Gable. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information Gable and the third-party services it relies on collect, and how that information is used. The app does not require you to create an account.

The short version: everything Gable is actually for — your home profile, your maintenance plan, your completed-task history, the notes and costs you record and any photos you attach — is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Gable has no server of its own. The only information that leaves your phone is what Google Play and the services listed in Section 3 collect for billing, crash reporting and anonymous usage analytics.

1. Your Home, Your Plan and Your History

When you set up Gable you answer a few questions about your home (its climate, its hemisphere and which features it has, such as gutters or a garage door). Gable uses those answers, entirely on your device, to build a maintenance plan from a task library that ships inside the app. Nothing about your home is sent anywhere to produce that plan, and Gable never asks for or collects your address or your location.

As you mark tasks done, Gable records the date and, if you choose to add them, a note, a cost and up to three photos. All of this is stored locally in the app's database and in the app's private storage on your device only. It is never uploaded as part of saving it. You can delete any individual entry, or erase your home, your plan, your history and every stored photo at once, from the app's Settings at any time.

Gable can export your history as a PDF or a CSV file. That export is created on your device and goes only where you send it using Android's own share sheet. We never receive a copy.

2. Permissions

Gable requests notification permission so it can remind you before a maintenance window closes. Reminders about your plan are generated and scheduled entirely on your device from the plan stored there. Separately, Gable can receive occasional messages sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging and Firebase In-App Messaging (Google) — for example that a new season has started or that a free trial is about to end. Those messages are written by us and sent from Google's servers; none of them carries your home profile, your task history, your notes, your costs or your photos, which stay on your device. Messages from Gable arrive on their own notification channel, "From Gable", which you can mute in Android's settings without muting your reminders. You can decline the permission or turn reminders off later, and the rest of the app continues to work.

When you attach a photo to a completed task, Gable uses the Android photo picker. The picker hands the app only the specific image you chose — the app is not granted access to the rest of your photo library, and Gable declares no storage or camera permission at all.

Gable does not request location, contacts, microphone or exact-alarm permissions, and it does not collect the Android Advertising ID (the permission is stripped from the app and Analytics ad-id collection is switched off in code).

3. Third-Party Services

Gable uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described below. These services are operated by their respective providers under their own privacy policies.

  • Firebase Analytics (Google): collects app usage and event data (e.g. which features are used, screens viewed, whether onboarding was completed) to help us understand how the app is used and improve it. This data is associated with a device/app-instance identifier, not your name or email. It does not include your home profile, your task history, your notes, your costs or your photos. We do not collect the Advertising ID.
    Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google): collects crash reports, including device information (model, OS version) and stack traces, to help us diagnose and fix bugs.
    Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google): fetches a small set of configuration values (for example the address of this policy) so we can adjust the app without shipping an update. It sends a device/app-instance identifier in order to receive those values; it does not send your content.
    Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): delivers the occasional messages described in Section 2. To do so, Google generates a registration token and a Firebase installation identifier on your device and records the app's version. Gable does not store the token anywhere, does not send messages to individual devices, and never includes your content in a message.
    Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Firebase In-App Messaging (Google): shows a message inside the app on some occasions (for example after a free trial ends) and records whether that message was shown, tapped or dismissed, associated with the same device/app-instance identifier as Firebase Analytics. It does not receive your content.
    Firebase Privacy and Security
  • RevenueCat: if you subscribe to Gable Pro, RevenueCat processes your purchase and entitlement status (e.g. whether Pro is active) so the app can unlock it, and generates an anonymous app-user id for that purpose. RevenueCat does not receive your home profile, your history or your photos.
    RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing: if you subscribe to Gable Pro, the purchase itself (including your payment method and billing details) is handled entirely by Google Play. Gable and its developer never see or store your payment information.
    Google Privacy Policy

Google (for Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Cloud Messaging and In-App Messaging) and RevenueCat each act as independent data processors under their own standard Data Processing Agreements, which apply automatically to all customers using their services — we do not need a separately negotiated DPA with either of them.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA/UK Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the UK, or another jurisdiction requiring a stated legal basis, we (and our processors) rely on:

  • Performance of a contract — for RevenueCat and Google Play Billing, to process your Pro subscription and grant the corresponding entitlement;
  • Legitimate interests — for Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config, to understand app usage, diagnose crashes and keep the app configurable so we can maintain and improve it; and for Firebase Cloud Messaging and In-App Messaging, to tell you about the app itself (a new season, a trial ending) — you can mute or decline these at any time.

We do not rely on consent as the legal basis for analytics or crash reporting; where local law requires opt-in consent for non-essential analytics (e.g. under ePrivacy rules), we will provide a consent mechanism before those services activate.

5. International Data Transfers

The third-party services listed above (Google/Firebase, RevenueCat) may process and store data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. These providers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (and, for Google, certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework) as the legal mechanism for these transfers. By using the app, you acknowledge this transfer. Your home profile, plan, history and photos are not part of any such transfer, because they never leave your device.

6. Data Retention

We retain data collected by our third-party services for only as long as necessary:

  • Your home profile, plan, history, notes, costs and photos: not retained by us at all; they exist only on your device, for as long as you keep them, and are erased when you delete them in the app or uninstall the app;
  • Firebase Analytics: event and user-level data is retained for 14 months from collection, matching Firebase's default retention setting;
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are retained for 90 days;
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging and In-App Messaging: the registration token and installation identifier exist for as long as the app is installed and are invalidated when it is uninstalled; message impression data follows the Firebase Analytics retention above;
  • RevenueCat / purchase records: your entitlement status is retained for as long as you have the app installed and, after that, for up to 7 years to satisfy standard financial and tax record-keeping obligations.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over the data collected about you by the third-party services described above. The content you create in Gable is already entirely under your own control on your device: you can view it, correct it, export it as a PDF or CSV, and delete it, all from within the app and without asking anyone.

If you are in the EEA, UK, or a similar jurisdiction (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the data held about you;
  • Correct inaccurate data;
  • Request erasure of your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • Restrict or object to certain processing;
  • Receive your data in a portable format;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

If you are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected, used, and disclosed;
  • Request deletion of your personal information;
  • Correct inaccurate personal information;
  • Non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights.

If you are in Brazil (LGPD — Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados), you have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we (or our processors) hold data about you, and access it;
  • Correct incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data;
  • Request anonymization, blocking, or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data, or data processed without a valid legal basis;
  • Receive your data in a portable format;
  • Be informed of the public and private entities with which we share your data (see Third-Party Services above);
  • Revoke consent, where consent is the legal basis relied on, and request deletion of data processed on that basis;
  • Lodge a complaint with the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD).

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is no "opt-out of sale/share" to exercise.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below; we aim to respond within 30 days (15 days for LGPD access requests, extendable once with justification). You may also exercise rights over a specific processor's own data directly with that processor (links in the Third-Party Services section above). As a solo developer with only occasional, low-risk processing of the kind described in this policy, we rely on the Article 27 GDPR exemption from appointing a dedicated EU representative.

8. Children's Privacy

Gable is a general-audience app for people who look after a home. It is not directed at children, its Google Play target audience does not include children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, where that age is higher).

The app is in any case built to collect very little from anyone, whatever their age:

  • There is no account, no sign-up and no field anywhere in the app for a name, an email address, a phone number, an age or any other contact detail, and there is no chat, no comments and no user-to-user contact of any kind.
  • The app shows no advertising. It does not collect the Android Advertising ID, and no one's data is used for behavioural advertising, ad targeting or profiling. Every notification the app shows is either a maintenance reminder generated on your own device or a message from us about the app itself, never an advertisement and never containing personal data.
  • The only identifiers involved are the device/app-instance identifier used by Firebase and the anonymous app-user id used by RevenueCat, described in Section 3. They are used only to keep the app working, diagnose crashes, understand aggregate usage and verify a subscription.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that information has been collected from your child through the app, contact us at the email in Section 11 and we will delete it.

9. Security

Your home profile, plan, history and photos live only in the app's private storage on your device, where other apps cannot read them. Files you export are written to the app's temporary cache and shared only with the app you choose in the share sheet, using a one-time read permission. Data collected by the third-party services above is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by their own security measures; no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
