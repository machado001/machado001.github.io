Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 5, 2026

Thank you for using Watch Identifier. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information Watch Identifier and the third-party services it relies on collect, and how that information is used. The app does not require you to create an account.

1. The Photo You Scan

When you identify a watch, the photo is sent to our own server-side relay (a Cloudflare Worker that we control) and forwarded from there to Anthropic's Claude API, which analyzes the image and returns the identification (brand, model, reference, estimated value). We do not store your photos on our servers — the relay holds the photo only for the duration of that one request and does not log or retain the image itself. Anthropic processes the photo to generate the result under its own Privacy Policy and API Terms.
Anthropic Privacy Policy

If you choose to save a result to your Collection, the photo and the identification details are stored locally in the app's database on your device only. They are never uploaded anywhere as part of that save, and you can delete any collection item, or the whole collection, from within the app at any time.

Each scan request also carries two anonymous identifiers — a one-way SHA-256 hash of your device's Android ID, and the anonymous app-user id our billing provider generates — and your IP address, which the relay necessarily sees as part of the connection. The relay uses them for three things only: counting how many free scans a device has already used, checking whether a Pro subscription is active, and blocking automated abuse. They are not a name, an email address or an account, they are never combined with an advertising identifier, and they are not used to build a profile of you or to send you anything.

2. Permissions

The app requests camera access to take a photo of a watch, and may request access to your photo gallery to pick an existing image instead. These permissions are used strictly to obtain the image you choose to identify.

3. Third-Party Services

Besides Anthropic (above), Watch Identifier uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described below. These services are operated by their respective providers under their own privacy policies.

  • Firebase Analytics (Google): collects app usage and event data (e.g. which features are used, screens viewed) to help us understand how the app is used and improve it. This data is associated with a device/app-instance identifier, not your name or email. We do not collect the Advertising ID.
    Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google): collects crash reports, including device information (model, OS version) and stack traces, to help us diagnose and fix bugs.
    Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): used to send occasional notifications (e.g. reminders about your collection). The app does not store or transmit your device's messaging token anywhere; notifications are sent as a broadcast, not targeted to individual users, in the current version.
    Firebase Privacy and Security
  • RevenueCat: if you purchase Watch Identifier Pro, RevenueCat processes your purchase and entitlement status (e.g. whether Pro is active) so the app can unlock unlimited scans. RevenueCat does not receive your photos or identification results.
    RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing: if you purchase Watch Identifier Pro, the purchase itself (including your payment method and billing details) is handled entirely by Google Play. Watch Identifier and its developer never see or store your payment information.
    Google Privacy Policy

Google (for Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Cloud Messaging), RevenueCat, and Anthropic each act as independent data processors under their own standard Data Processing Agreements, which apply automatically to all customers using their services — we do not need a separately negotiated DPA with any of them.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA/UK Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the UK, or another jurisdiction requiring a stated legal basis, we (and our processors) rely on:

  • Performance of a contract — for Anthropic and our relay, to generate the watch identification you requested; and for RevenueCat and Google Play Billing, to process your Pro purchase and grant the corresponding entitlement;
  • Legitimate interests — for Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, to understand app usage and diagnose crashes so we can maintain and improve the App.

We do not rely on consent as the legal basis for analytics or crash reporting; where local law requires opt-in consent for non-essential analytics (e.g. under ePrivacy rules), we will provide a consent mechanism before those services activate.

5. International Data Transfers

The third-party services listed above (Anthropic, Google/Firebase, RevenueCat) may process and store data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. These providers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (and, for Google, certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework) as the legal mechanism for these transfers. By using the App, you acknowledge this transfer.

6. Data Retention

We retain data collected by our third-party services for only as long as necessary:

  • Your scanned photos: not retained by our relay or by us at all; a copy exists only on your device, and only for results you chose to save to your Collection, until you delete them;
  • Free-scan and abuse counters: the counter keyed to the hashed device id and anonymous app-user id is kept only for as long as it takes to enforce the free-scan quota for that device; the IP-based abuse counter expires automatically after 24 hours;
  • Firebase Analytics: event and user-level data is retained for 14 months from collection, matching Firebase's default retention setting;
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are retained for 90 days;
  • RevenueCat / purchase records: your entitlement status is retained for as long as you have the App installed and, after that, for up to 7 years to satisfy standard financial and tax record-keeping obligations.

8. Children's Privacy

Watch Identifier is a general-audience app. It is not directed at children, its Google Play target audience does not include children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, where that age is higher).

The App is in any case built to collect very little from anyone, whatever their age:

  • There is no account, no sign-up and no field anywhere in the App for a name, an email address, a phone number, an age or any other contact detail, and there is no chat, no comments and no user-to-user contact of any kind.
  • The App shows no advertising. It does not collect the Android Advertising ID (the permission is stripped from the App and Analytics ad-id collection is switched off in code), and no one's data is used for behavioural advertising, ad targeting or profiling. Any notification we send is a reminder about the App itself, never an advertisement.
  • The identifiers described in Sections 1 and 3 are used only to enforce the free-scan quota, verify a subscription, prevent abuse, keep the App working and diagnose crashes.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that information has been collected from your child through the App, contact us at the email in Section 11 and we will delete it.

9. Security

Your scanned photos are transmitted to our relay and to Anthropic over encrypted connections and are not stored server-side. Saved Collection items live only in the app's local database on your device. Data collected by the third-party services above is protected by their own security measures; no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
